Owlit Data Deletion Instructions

At Owlit, we respect your privacy and allow you to delete your data at any time. Follow these steps to remove your data from our platform:

1. Deauthorize Owlit from Instagram

  • Go to your Instagram settings.
  • Navigate to Apps and Websites under the Security section.
  • Find Owlit in the list of active apps.
  • Click Remove to deauthorize Owlit.

2. Data Deletion from Owlit

  • After deauthorizing, visit the Owlit app at https://home.owlit.in.
  • Log in using your Google account.
  • Go to the Instagram section in the app.
  • Click on the Deauthorize button to delete all associated data (e.g., synced posts, metadata, tokens) from our system.

3. Track the Deletion Process (Optional)

If you need confirmation of your data deletion request, please contact our support team at support@owlit.in for assistance.
